# Data and media sources

- **MovieLens 100K** was produced by GroupLens Research at the University of Minnesota. The downloader verifies the official archive against a pinned SHA-256 digest. Follow the upstream terms before use, including acknowledgement, limits on redistribution and commercial use, and applicable licensing terms. Raw ratings, movie metadata, and fitted research artifacts stay in ignored local files. This repository includes only aggregate evaluation outputs. [Dataset and terms](https://grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/100k/) · [Official dataset readme](https://files.grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/ml-100k-README.txt)
- **Fictional catalog** contains 30 original imagined scenes generated with OpenAI image generation for this project. FFmpeg adds slow camera motion to each still. The clips are silent, carry English visual-description caption tracks, and are labeled simulated. Catalog titles and descriptions are fictional. There are no studio posters, streamed platform clips, recognizable fictional properties, or licensed sound recordings.
- **Code** is released under the repository’s [MIT license](LICENSE). Dependencies carry their own published licenses.
